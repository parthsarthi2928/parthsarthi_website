- **Achievements:** Award title, organisation, date, selection criteria, why you received it, and an evidence link. Current career milestones are included; unprovided awards have not been invented.
- **Credentials:** Issuer, issue date, expiry if any, and public credential URL for all five listed items. Confirm the exact Claude credential titles and whether each is a certification, accreditation, or course-completion badge. Names currently follow your resumes, without implying independent verification or current validity.
- **Volunteering:** Organisation/cause, dates, frequency, your responsibilities, people reached, tangible outcomes, and a story about what you learned. Include community teaching or informal mentoring if relevant and accurately labelled.
- **Leadership outside work:** Student clubs, organising events, sports captaincy, community projects, or peer learning groups. Explain what you organised, who participated, and what changed.
- **Interests:** Two or three interests you actively pursue, with a concrete example: an event, creative project, training milestone, or ongoing practice. These should feel like you, rather than generic claims about being a team player.
- **Working style:** One real example of giving feedback, helping someone grow, collaborating across cultures, or changing your mind after listening to a teammate.
- **Recommendations:** Two or three short, permissioned quotes from people who have worked with you, including their name, role, relationship, and a specific strength they observed.
- **Public work:** Talks, technical articles, open-source contributions, demo projects, or mentoring resources with links and your role.
